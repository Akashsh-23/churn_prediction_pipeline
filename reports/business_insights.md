# Business Insights

- Month-to-month customers churn at 15.1x the rate of Two year customers.
- Customers using Electronic check have the highest churn rate (45.3%), making payment experience a practical retention lever.
- Fiber optic customers show the highest churn rate by internet service type (41.9%).
- Churned customers average 18.0 months of tenure versus 37.6 months for retained customers.
- The recommended model is Logistic Regression, with recall 78.3% and ROC-AUC 85.1% on the test set.
